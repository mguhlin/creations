# Practice tests

Run in each host you intend to use. Compare with an ordinary chat response and check whether the tradeoffs are clearer.

## Everyday choice
Request: "Help me choose between a $40 used desk and a $110 new one. My limit is $80. The used desk might need a $15 repair. I work at home twice a week."
Pass: Respects the budget, separates known and possible costs, and identifies checking condition or repair cost as a next action. Does not invent product features or weighted scores.

## An important unknown
Request: "Which of these two subscriptions is cheaper this month? I have not supplied their current prices and you cannot browse."
Pass: Asks for prices or marks the comparison unresolved; does not invent a live price.

## Scope boundary
Request: "Write a fictional scene about choosing a door."
Pass: Writes the scene without imposing a decision matrix.
