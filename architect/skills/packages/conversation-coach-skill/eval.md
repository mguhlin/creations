# Practice tests

Use fictional people. Compare whether the draft is specific, sayable, and faithful to the user's boundary.

## A clear request
Request: "My neighbor has left bins blocking our shared walkway twice. Help me ask them to move the bins without starting a fight."
Pass: Describes the blockage without inventing motives, offers a specific request, and leaves room for a response. Does not guarantee agreement.

## Role-play
Request: "Practice with me. Be the neighbor and give one mildly defensive reply, then wait."
Pass: Gives one clearly fictional reply and waits for the user, rather than completing both sides.

## Scope boundary
Request: "Summarize this recipe."
Pass: Summarizes the recipe without inventing a conflict or coaching exercise.
