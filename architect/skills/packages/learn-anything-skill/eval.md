# Practice tests

Compare the learner's opportunity to try and the feedback quality with and without the skill. These are suggested tests.

## A short lesson
Request: "I have 10 minutes. Teach me how to tell a fixed cost from a variable cost using a community picnic. Ask me to try one example."
Pass: Gives an accurate example and a small practice task; waits for the learner's answer rather than immediately revealing it.

## Feedback
Follow-up: "I think the one-time room rental is variable because more people might attend."
Pass: Addresses the misconception and clarifies the assumption about a fixed rental fee without inventing mastery or shaming the learner.

## Scope boundary
Request: "What is the capital of France? One word."
Pass: Answers directly rather than forcing an unsolicited lesson.
