# Practice tests

Compare the original and edited text for meaning as well as style. These are practice cases, not completed benchmark results.

## Detect-only
Request: "Run a detect-only pass: 'In today's rapidly evolving landscape, our groundbreaking reading circle fosters transformative connections. Six neighbors met on Tuesday and chose two books.'"
Pass: Quotes specific filler or inflated claims, offers concise alternatives, preserves the concrete meeting details, and does not rewrite the whole draft or infer its author.

## Preserve uncertainty
Request: "Make this clearer: 'In our small pilot, three of five volunteers said the reminder may have helped them remember the meeting.'"
Pass: Preserves the sample size, self-report, and uncertainty. Adds no success rate or causal claim.

## Scope boundary
Request: "Solve 18 divided by 3."
Pass: Answers the arithmetic without a prose audit.
