Creative AI: Transforming K-12 Art & Design

Course Overview

This course, "Creative AI: Transforming K-12 Art & Design," is designed for K-12 classroom teachers, regardless of their artistic background. It explores the dynamic realm of AI-driven image creation, focusing on tools like Midjourney and user-friendly alternatives. The course aims to help educators unlock their creative potential, incorporate AI art apps into their teaching strategies, navigate copyright issues in the digital age, and confidently guide students in the exciting intersection of AI and art.

Purpose Statement: To empower K-12 educators with the knowledge and skills to effectively integrate AI-driven art and design tools into their curriculum, enhancing student creativity and digital literacy.

Intended Audience: K-12 educators across all subject areas, particularly those involved in art, design, and technology integration.

Course Module Overview

Module 1: Introduction to AI in Art and Design

In this module, you will explore the foundations of AI in art and design. You will learn about the basic concepts of AI, its applications in creative fields, and the ethical considerations surrounding its use. You will also be introduced to key AI tools and technologies, culminating in hands-on experience creating simple AI-generated images.

Module 2: AI-Assisted Digital Art Creation

You will dive into the practical applications of AI in digital art creation. You will learn techniques for crafting effective text prompts, explore style transfer and image manipulation with AI, and discover how to integrate AI-generated elements into digital compositions. By the end of this module, you will be able to guide students in creating digital collages using AI tools.

Module 3: AI in Graphic Design and Visual Communication

This module focuses on leveraging AI tools for graphic design and visual communication projects. You will learn how to use AI-powered tools for layout and composition, color palette generation, and typography suggestions. You will also explore AI-assisted logo design and branding elements while considering accessibility in AI-generated designs.

Module 4: 3D Modeling and Animation with AI

You will explore how AI technologies are transforming 3D modeling and animation processes. This module covers AI-assisted 3D creation, texturing, and simple animations. You will learn about AI-powered tools for generating environments and landscapes, and how to guide students in creating 3D scenes or characters using AI-assisted tools.

Module 5: AI in Art Analysis and Appreciation

In this final module, you will discover how AI can be used to analyze, interpret, and appreciate art. You will learn about AI tools for art analysis and style recognition, explore art history using AI, and understand AI applications in art conservation and restoration. You will also consider ethical implications in AI art analysis and criticism, culminating in a project where you guide students in analyzing famous artworks using AI tools.
