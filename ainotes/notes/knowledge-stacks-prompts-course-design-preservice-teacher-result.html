Course Overview

This online, self-paced course is designed to prepare preservice teachers for success in K-12 schools. The purpose is to ensure preservice teachers are ready to teach by covering critical elements of teaching, including classroom management, lesson design, instructional strategies, and more. The course blends technology into every aspect, addressing areas that many traditional preservice courses may not cover.

Intended Audience: Preservice teachers and universities looking to gain practical insights for K-12 teaching.

Course Module Overview

Module 1: Introduction to Teaching

You will explore an overview of the teaching profession, its importance, and the role of teachers in society. This involves understanding the roles and responsibilities of teachers, collaborative roles within educational systems, and qualities of effective teaching.

Module 2: Educational Psychology

You will learn about theories and principles of learning psychology, explore factors that influence student learning, and discover how to create a positive learning environment. You will also learn effective techniques to motivate and engage students.

Module 3: Classroom Management

You will learn the skills and strategies needed to manage a classroom effectively, including how to prevent and resolve conflicts, create rules, communicate effectively, and build positive relationships with students.

Module 4: Lesson Design and Planning

You will explore various lesson design and planning strategies, including the 5E/6E Model, Design Thinking, and Engineering Design Process. Special attention is given to clarifying lesson objectives, content objectives, and social objectives in lesson plans.

Module 5: Instructional Strategies and Assessment

You will learn about high-effect size instructional strategies, how to craft formative and summative assessments, use assessment data to inform future instruction, and provide constructive feedback to students.
