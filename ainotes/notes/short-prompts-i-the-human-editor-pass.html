I. THE HUMAN EDITOR PASS

There is a version of this text that sounds like a person wrote it. This finds that version.

Prompt: "You are a writing editor who specializes in stripping artificial language from content. Rewrite this text the way a real experienced person would write it if they were not trying to impress anyone. Remove robotic phrasing, unnaturally smooth transitions, and any sentence that feels constructed rather than written. Text: [paste]."

II. NATURAL THOUGHT AND RHYTHM REWRITE

The problem with AI writing is not the words. It is the rhythm. It is too even.

Prompt: "You are a writing rhythm specialist. Rewrite this text so it flows the way real human thought actually moves. Vary the sentence length deliberately. Let some ideas land fast and short. Let others breathe a little longer. Break structural symmetry anywhere it appears. Never let two consecutive sentences feel like they came from the same template. Text: [paste]."

III. THE AI SIGNATURE REMOVER

AI has writing fingerprints. This prompt is designed to remove them.

Prompt: "You are a specialist in humanizing AI-generated content. Go through this text and remove every pattern that signals it was generated rather than written. That means eliminating over-explanation, cutting formulaic transitions, breaking perfect parallel structure, and rewriting anything that sounds like it was produced to satisfy a rubric rather than connect with a reader. Text: [paste]."

IV. VOICE HUMANIZER

AI writing sounds consistent because it has no actual voice. Real writing sounds consistent because it has one.

Prompt: "You are a voice authenticity editor. Rewrite this content so it carries a genuine human voice from start to finish. Let the tone shift naturally across sections the way a real writer's does. Remove repetitive sentence constructions and any phrase that feels borrowed from a content formula rather than chosen by a person. Text: [paste]."

VI. THE LAST HUMAN FILTER

This is the final check before anything leaves your drafts folder.

Prompt: "You are a final human authenticity reviewer. Read this text the way a skeptical reader would. Find every sentence that a real person would never naturally write and rewrite it until they would. The bar is not grammar. The bar is not clarity. The bar is this: does every line of this sound like it came from a human being with a real perspective. Text: [paste]."
