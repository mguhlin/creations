Wish you had an easy to understand and apply set of expectations for technology in education? The Texas Computer Education Association (TCEA) has introduced Essential Learning Expectations (ELEs) to guide educators in preparing students for the modern world. Let’s explore briefly how these ELEs can transform professional learning in schools.

<img width="780" height="585" src="../../../_resources/TCEA-Essential-Learning-Expectat_1e9b2161b3464d098.png"/>

Embracing Technology Integration

Technology integration is a cornerstone of the TCEA ELEs. Teachers are expected to seamlessly incorporate technology into their practices, including the use of AI tools to enhance student learning. TCEA offers student AI literacy frameworks, as well as a specific ELE for AI in Education.

Classroom Scenario: Mrs. Rodriguez uses AI-powered writing assistants in her English class. “By integrating these tools, students not only improve their essays but also learn to critically evaluate AI-generated content,” she explains.

Did You Know?
TCEA is available to provide professional learning on the topics covered in the TCEA Essential Learning Expectations. You can also [access a list of TCEA ELEs aligned online, self-paced courses](https://courses.tcea.org).

Fostering Digital Literacy

The ELEs emphasize the importance of digital literacy, particularly in evaluating online information and combating misinformation.

Classroom Scenario: Mr. Chen guides his students through a fact-checking exercise. “We’re learning to verify online sources and recognize credible information. It’s crucial in today’s digital age,” he notes.

<img width="780" height="585" src="../../../_resources/Teachers-3.0-Inclusive-Teaching_ac1270a5ec144f81a5.png"/>

Promoting Inclusive Learning Environments

Creating inclusive classrooms that respect diversity is a key focus of the ELEs.

Classroom Scenario: Ms. Thompson implements culturally responsive teaching methods. “By valuing diverse backgrounds, we create an environment where all students feel engaged and motivated to learn,” she shares.

Essential Learning Expectations

The TCEA Essential Learning Expectations provide a comprehensive framework for educators to create engaging, effective, and inclusive learning environments. By focusing on technology integration, digital literacy, and inclusive teaching, these ELEs prepare students for success in an increasingly complex world.
