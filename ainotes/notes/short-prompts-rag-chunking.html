Universal Chunking Instructions (Production-Ready)

Goal:

Transform any long-form content into coherent, self-contained chunks optimized for retrieval accuracy, semantic clarity, and minimal hallucination risk.

⸻

  1. General Rules

When chunking any text:

  1. Preserve meaning.

Each chunk must represent a complete idea, section, or logical unit.

  1. Avoid cutting mid-thought.

Never break across sentences, clause boundaries, lists, or examples.

  1. Target chunk size:

300–800 tokens (shorter for dense text, longer for narrative).

  1. Maintaining context:

Use overlap of 50–150 tokens when content has dependencies across sections.

  1. Attach metadata:

For every chunk include:

• source_title

• chunk_id

• section_heading (if available)

• token_count

• summary (1–2 sentences)

⸻

  1. Chunking Strategy (Apply in Order)

Step 1. Hierarchical Parsing (Structure First)

Use the document’s natural structure if it exists.

Break at:

• Headings

• Subheadings

• Paragraph groups

• Numbered sections

• Bullet clusters

Output: preliminary chunks respecting document structure.

⸻

Step 2. Meaning Check (Semantic Integrity)

For each preliminary chunk:

• Ensure it expresses a complete concept.

• If it contains multiple unrelated themes → split it.

• If chunks fall below 150 tokens, merge with nearest related neighbor.

⸻

Step 3. Semantic Refinement (Meaning-Based Boundaries)

Evaluate chunk boundaries using semantic similarity:

• Do not separate text where concepts are tightly linked.

• Do separate text where topic changes abruptly.

• Adjust boundaries so each chunk has a clear topical focus.

⸻

Step 4. Windowed Overlap (Context Preservation)

Apply overlap only when needed, such as:

• Legal text

• Technical procedures

• Stepwise instructions

• Research analysis

Use 50–150 token overlap to preserve context.

⸻

Step 5. Token Normalization

Ensure final chunks are sized for efficient retrieval:

• <250 tokens → expand with context if meaningful

• 900 tokens → split at the nearest semantic boundary

Goal: consistent chunk sizes without damaging meaning.

⸻

  1. Special Handling Rules (Based on Content Type)

A. Technical Documentation

• Prioritize hierarchical boundaries

• Preserve lists, tables, definitions

• Use small overlaps between procedural steps

B. Legal Contracts

• Chunk at clause boundaries

• Never break definitions or terms-of-art

• Apply generous overlap (100–150 tokens)

C. Research Papers

• Use semantic chunking

• Keep methodology, results, and discussion separate

• Ensure charts/tables remain with their explanations

D. Blog Posts / Articles

• Chunk by topic or argument shift

• Avoid overlaps unless there is narrative flow

E. Multi-Topic Reports

• Use topic-boundary detection

• Keep each chunk focused on a single theme

⸻

  1. Output Format Template

Produce chunks in the following structure:

CHUNK {number}

source_title: {title}

section: {heading or topic}

token_count: {approx count}

content:

"""

{chunk text}

"""

summary:

"{one or two sentence summary capturing the meaning of the chunk}"

⸻

  1. Validation Checklist

Before finalizing, verify that:

• Each chunk stands alone without requiring unseen context

• All chunks together preserve full document meaning

• No concept is split awkwardly

• Metadata is complete

• Chunk sizes fall within target ranges
