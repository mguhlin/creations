Prompt for Generating Content for the Conference Submission Form:

---

Topic and Brief Description:

Please provide the topic of your session and a brief description (1-2 sentences) outlining the main focus or objective.

---

1. Presenter's Information:

  • Primary Presenter's Name:

Enter the full name of the primary presenter.

  • Primary Presenter's Job Title:

Enter the official job title of the primary presenter.

  • Primary Presenter's Company/ISD:

Enter the name of the organization, school district, or company where the primary presenter is employed.

  • Primary Presenter's Preferred Email:

Enter the preferred email address for communication.

  • Primary Presenter's Alternate Email (Optional):

Enter an alternate email address, if available.

  • Primary Presenter's Cell Phone:

Enter a mobile phone number for direct contact.

  • Names of Co-Presenter(s):

List the names, job titles, organizations, and email addresses of any co-presenters, if applicable.

---

2. Session Information:

  • Session Title:

Craft a concise and engaging title for your session (10 words maximum).

  • Session Description:

Write a brief overview of your session that summarizes its content and goals.

  • Detailed Description:

Provide a more in-depth explanation of the session, highlighting key points, objectives, and what participants can expect to learn or achieve (100 words maximum).

---

3. Session Details:

  • Strand:

Select the most appropriate category for your session from the provided options (e.g., Apple Implementation, Artificial Intelligence, Network Management, etc.).

  • Presentation Length:

Choose the duration of your session (50 minutes or 1 hour 50 minutes).

  • Type of Session:

Select the format of your session: Presentation, Panel, or Sponsor/Partner Session.

  • Skill Level:

Indicate the level of knowledge required for participants: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.

---

4. Additional Information:

  • Other Information/Comments:

Provide any additional comments or information that might be relevant to the session proposal.

---

How to Use the Prompt:

  1. Input the topic and brief description.

This will help generate the session title, description, and detailed description.

  1. Fill in the presenter's details.

Ensure accurate and complete information for all presenters.

  1. Generate session-specific content.

The prompt will help create the session title, description, and detailed explanation tailored to your topic.

  1. Select appropriate options for session details.

Choose the strand, session length, type, and skill level based on the content and audience.

  1. Add any other relevant information or comments.

This ensures all critical details are captured for the submission.
