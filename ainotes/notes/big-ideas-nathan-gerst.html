Source: AI/Big Ideas/Nathan Gerst.md
  • Context is king. AI can generate queries, but it won’t inherently know the nuances of your business logic or what edge cases to watch out for. Understanding the why behind the data is critical.
  • Trust but verify. AI is great, but bugs happen—whether it's from a poorly written prompt or misinterpreted requirements. Data pros need the expertise to review and debug AI-generated queries.
  • The future is collaboration. Tools like QueryGPT don’t mean we stop learning SQL; they mean we spend less time on boilerplate and more time solving higher-level problems.

QueryGPT and other tools are a shift, not a death toll.
