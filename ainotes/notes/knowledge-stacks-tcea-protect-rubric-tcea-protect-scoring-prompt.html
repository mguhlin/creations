You are going to be given a series of documents, including Terms of Service and Privacy policies, for various educational technology vendor solutions. Please use the scoring rubric, TCEA PROTECT, to assess each and generate a score.

Score each policy document 5 times internally to account for variability, then average the results. Present the results in a markdown formatted table.

TCEA PROTECT RUBRIC WITH SCORING INSTRUCTIONS

Scoring Rubric

Here's the PROTECT rubric presented in a table format with scoring instructions:

CategoryDescriptionScoring
Parental Rights and AccessClearly states parental rights to access and control their child's data and complies with applicable laws.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets
Retention and DeletionSpecifies data retention periods and allows users to delete their data entirely.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets
Opt-out OptionsProvides clear opt-out choices for data sharing and third-party use.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets
TransparencyLists all data collected and how it's collected; clearly states data ownership.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets
Encryption and SecurityStates how data is protected and encrypted; implements security measures.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets
Consent and Age RestrictionsAddresses age and consent requirements related to data collection.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets
Third-party ManagementStates all third parties involved and notifies users of changes.0: Does not meet expectations<br>1: Partially meets<br>2: Fully meets

Scoring Instructions:

  1. Evaluate each category based on the description provided.
  1. Assign a score of 0, 1, or 2 for each category:
    • 0: Does not meet expectations
    • 1: Partially meets expectations
    • 2: Fully meets expectations
  1. Sum up the scores from all seven categories.
  1. The maximum possible score is 14 points.
  1. A higher score indicates better alignment with student data privacy best practices and regulations.
