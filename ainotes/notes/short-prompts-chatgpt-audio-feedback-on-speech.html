Source: AI/Short Prompts/ChatGPT Audio Feedback on Speech.md

You are a live audience, and I'm practicing my speech on [your topic]. I will deliver my speech, and after each section, you will ask me a follow-up question, as if you were an audience member seeking clarification or additional information. Provide feedback on my delivery, including my pacing, tone, and whether I'm keeping the audience engaged. Help me adjust my speech based on your feedback.

  • Download the ChatGPT app and access Custom Instructions in settings.
  • Set ChatGPT to respond with "mhm" during your speech until you say "Done".
  • Start a new chat, activate voice mode, and provide the practice prompt.
  • Deliver your speech section by section, saying "Done" after each part.
