Course Title: AI-Powered Teaching: Mastering Your Classroom with BoodleBox Unlimited

Course Overview

This online, self-paced course is designed for K-12 educators seeking to master the use of AI in their classrooms. Using the BoodleBox Unlimited platform, you will progress from foundational skills to advanced applications. You will learn to save time on lesson planning, create differentiated materials, and design engaging student projects. You will also learn to manage the full cycle of student work—from creation to assessment—using BoodleBox's integrated tools, ensuring a secure and collaborative environment.

Course Module Overview

Module 1: Foundations of AI in Education with BoodleBox

In this module, you will learn the fundamentals of BoodleBox Unlimited. You will explore the user interface, understand the value of using multiple premium AI models, and practice writing effective prompts to get started. This foundational knowledge will set you up for success in integrating AI into your daily teaching practice.

Module 2: AI-Powered Lesson Planning and Content Creation

In this module, you will dive into practical applications for your own workload. You will use BoodleBox to brainstorm ideas, generate and rewrite lesson plans to meet specific standards, differentiate instructional materials for diverse learners, and create engaging content for your students.

Module 3: Engaging Students and Streamlining Assessment with AI

In this module, you will shift your focus to the student-facing workflow. You will learn how to design AI-enhanced assignments that promote critical thinking, use AI to help create fair and consistent rubrics, and explore methods for formally assigning and evaluating student work within BoodleBox.

Module 4: Advanced BoodleBox: Collaboration and Customization

In this module, you will explore the advanced features of BoodleBox Unlimited. You will learn how to collaborate with colleagues in shared chats, stack multiple bots for complex problem-solving, and build your own custom BoodleBot tailored to a specific subject or classroom need, enhancing your productivity and professional learning.

Module 5: Leading with AI: Ethics, Safety, and School-Wide Integration

In this module, you will examine the bigger picture of AI in your school or district. You will learn about responsible AI implementation, data privacy compliance, and strategies for monitoring student use. You will also explore how to advocate for and support a school-wide AI initiative.

3.0 About
"Assessment is today’s means of modifying tomorrow’s instruction." (Carole Tomlinson, ASCD)

You have mastered using AI to lighten your own workload; now it is time to bring these powerful tools into your students' learning experiences. This module focuses on designing meaningful, AI-enhanced assignments and revolutionizing your assessment workflow. You will learn how to create tasks that build critical thinking skills and use BoodleBox to provide faster, fairer, and more effective feedback.

3.1 Explore

In this section, you will learn how to move students from being passive consumers of information to active partners with AI. We will explore how to design assignments where AI acts as a collaborator, not a shortcut. You will also learn how to generate clear and objective rubrics in seconds and use BoodleBox's integrated tools to formally assign, collect, and grade student work.

Capacity Matrix Items
  • I can design an assignment that requires students to use AI as a tool for learning.
  • I know how to use BoodleBox to generate a detailed rubric for an assignment.
  • I am aware of the importance of evaluating a student's AI process, not just their final product.
  • I can use BoodleBox to formally assign and collect student work.
  • I am able to use AI to provide personalized and constructive feedback on student work.

Problem

Your students have just completed a "Genius Hour" project where they researched a topic of their choice and created a five-minute presentation to share their findings. You need a fair way to assess their presentation skills and the depth of their research, and you want to provide specific, actionable feedback to each student.

Solution

[mod321.mp4]

This video demonstrates a two-step workflow for first creating a universal presentation rubric and then using BoodleBox Assignments to have students submit their outlines and research notes for feedback.

Script: _This is a perfect scenario for a two-step process. First, let's create a universal rubric for presentation skills. Now, we will create a BoodleBox Assignment called "Genius Hour Outline Submission." In the instructions, we will ask students to use BoodleBox to brainstorm their topic and create an outline. We will attach our rubric so they know the final expectations. When students submit the assignment, you can review their research process in their chat history and provide targeted feedback on their outline._
