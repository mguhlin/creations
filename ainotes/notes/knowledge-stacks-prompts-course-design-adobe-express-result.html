1.2.3 - Navigating the Adobe Express Interface

Explore the layout and main features of the Adobe Express workspace[mod123.mp4].

Script:

_Now that we're logged in, let's explore the Adobe Express interface. The main dashboard is your starting point for all projects. At the top, you'll see a search bar where you can look for templates or specific design elements. Below that, you'll find categories of templates to choose from. On the left sidebar, you'll see options for different types of projects like social media posts, presentations, and videos. When you start a new project, you'll enter the editing interface. Here, on the left, you have your toolbox with options for adding text, photos, icons, and more. The main canvas is in the center, where you'll do most of your design work. On the right, you'll find properties panels where you can adjust colors, fonts, and other elements. 1.2.4 - Creating Your First Project in Adobe Express

Step-by-step guide to creating a simple flyer using Adobe Express[mod124.mp4].

Script:

_Let's create your first project in Adobe Express – a simple flyer for a classroom event. Start by clicking "Create new" on your dashboard. From the template categories, select "Flyer." Browse through the options and choose a template that fits your event. Once you've selected a template, you'll enter the editing interface. To change the text, simply click on it and start typing. You can adjust the font, size, and color using the text properties panel on the right. To replace an image, click on it and select "Replace" from the options that appear. You can upload your own image or choose from Adobe's extensive library. To add new elements, use the left sidebar. You can add shapes, icons, or additional text boxes. Remember to maintain a clean, readable design. Once you're happy with your flyer, click the "Download" button at the top right to save your work. Congratulations! You've just created your first project in Adobe Express. With practice, you'll be creating professional-looking materials in no time._

2.2.5 - Accessibility Considerations in Administrative Documents

Understand how to make your Adobe Express creations accessible to all staff and community members[mod225.mp4].

Script:

_Accessibility is crucial in all aspects of education, including administrative documents. In this video, we'll explore how to make your Adobe Express creations accessible to all staff and community members. First, let's talk about color contrast. When choosing colors for your text and background, ensure there's enough contrast for easy readability. Adobe Express has a contrast checker tool to help with this. Next, consider font choices. Use clear, sans-serif fonts for better legibility, especially in digital formats. When adding images, always include alternative text descriptions. This allows screen readers to convey the image content to visually impaired users. For infographics and charts, provide the same information in text format as well. When creating videos or presentations, consider adding captions or transcripts. Finally, when sharing your documents digitally, ensure they're compatible with screen readers and other assistive technologies. By keeping these accessibility considerations in mind, you'll create administrative documents that are inclusive and accessible to all members of your school community._
