Recurring ThemesCourse: Crosscutting Concepts in Science<br><br>1. Structure and Function:<br>How does the structure of objects and living things relate to their function? Why is this relationship important across scientific disciplines? This module explores the fundamental connection between structure and function in various contexts.<br>- I can explain how the structure of an object or organism relates to its function.<br>- I understand how to apply structure-function relationships to solve problems.<br>- I can analyze complex systems in terms of their structural components and functions.<br><br>2. Stability and Change:<br>What factors contribute to stability in systems? How and why do systems change over time? Students will investigate the balance between stability and change in natural and designed systems.<br>- I can identify factors that contribute to stability in various systems.<br>- I understand how systems respond to changes and disturbances.<br>- I can explain the importance of feedback mechanisms in maintaining system stability.<br><br>3. Energy and Matter:<br>How are energy and matter conserved and transferred in various processes? What role do they play in system changes? This module focuses on the fundamental principles of energy and matter in scientific phenomena.<br>- I can describe how energy and matter are conserved in various processes.<br>- I understand the different forms of energy and their transformations.<br>- I can explain how energy and matter flow through natural and designed systems.<br><br>4. Systems and System Models:<br>What constitutes a system? How do scientists and engineers use models to understand complex systems? Students will learn about systems thinking and the use of models in scientific inquiry and engineering design.<br>- I can identify and describe the components and boundaries of various systems.<br>- I understand how to develop and use models to represent complex systems.<br>- I can analyze the interactions and feedbacks within and between systems.<br><br>5. Patterns and Cause and Effect:<br>How do scientists identify patterns in nature? How are patterns used to establish cause and effect relationships? This module explores the role of pattern recognition and causal reasoning in scientific thinking.<br>- I can identify and describe patterns in natural phenomena.<br>- I understand how to use patterns to make predictions and inferences.<br>- I can distinguish between correlation and causation in scientific contexts.
