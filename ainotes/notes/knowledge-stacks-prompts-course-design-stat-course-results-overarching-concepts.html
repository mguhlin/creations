Course Module Overview

Course Title: Foundations of Scientific Thinking

This course provides an overview of the overarching concepts in the new Science TEKS (Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills) framework. You will explore fundamental scientific principles, including patterns, cause and effect, scale, systems, and the relationship between energy and matter. The course is designed to help K-12 educators integrate these concepts into their science lessons effectively, making learning engaging and meaningful for students.

Module 1: Patterns in Nature

You will explore how patterns emerge in natural systems and understand the significance of these patterns in scientific understanding. This module will equip you with strategies to help students identify and interpret patterns in the natural world.

Module 2: Cause and Effect Relationships

Understanding the relationship between cause and effect is essential in science. In this module, you will learn how to help students distinguish between correlation and causation and how to design experiments that effectively demonstrate these relationships.

Module 3: Scale, Proportion, and Quantity

This module will focus on why scale and proportion are important in science and how they affect our understanding of the world. You will learn how changes in scale can reveal new insights and patterns in scientific observations.

Module 4: Systems and System Models

You will explore what constitutes a system in science and how models can help represent complex systems. This module will guide you in developing and using models to explain and predict scientific phenomena, enhancing students' systems thinking skills.

Module 5: Energy and Matter

In this module, you will examine the relationship between energy and matter, focusing on principles such as the conservation of energy and matter. You will explore how these principles apply in various scientific processes and how to effectively communicate these concepts to students.
