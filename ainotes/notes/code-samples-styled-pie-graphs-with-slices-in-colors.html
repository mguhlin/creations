In these graphs, the categories have been adjusted to:

  1. Have inner monologue
  1. Do not have (or have little) inner monologue

For the TCEA respondents, scores 4-5 are considered as having an inner monologue, while scores 0-3 are grouped together as not having (or having little) inner monologue.

The general population data remains the same, as it was already divided into "Have inner monologue" and "Do not have inner monologue" categories.

This adjustment allows for a more direct comparison between the TCEA respondents and the general population data. However, the caveat about the small sample size for TCEA respondents still applies.
