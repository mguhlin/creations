Prompt:

"I need to align the AI Literacy Framework for Students with the TCEA ELEs for AI in Education. Here are the details:

  1. TCEA ELEs for AI in Education: Please refer to the TCEA Essential Learning Expectations (ELEs) document for the specific expectations.
  1. AI Literacy Framework for Students:
    • Grades K-2 - Discover: Develop a basic understanding of what AI is and how it's used in daily life.
    • Grades 3-5 - Experiment: Begin to play with machine learning concepts and simple AI applications.
    • Grades 6-8 - Build: Explore more complex AI applications, AI ethics, bias, and societal impacts, and develop best practices for use.
    • Grades 9-12 - Innovate: Create new learning using advanced AI concepts, programming, and career exploration.
  1. Lesson Titles and Descriptions:
    • Grades K-2:
      • AI Animal Guessing Game: Recognize AI in everyday technology and understand that AI can follow instructions and make decisions.
      • If-Then Adventure: Introduce basic concepts of computer logic through storytelling and physical activities.
      • Asking Questions to an AI Helper: Practice asking questions using who, what, when, where, why, and how.
      • Practice Asking an AI Helper: Recognize that AI needs data to function and practice rephrasing questions if the AI doesn't understand.
    • Grades 3-5:
      • Teaching Machines to Learn: Explain what machine learning is and understand the concept of training data.
      • Data Detective Academy: Introduce basic data literacy concepts and data collection methods.
      • AI Context Detectives: Understand the importance of context in communication and practice crafting AI prompts.
      • AI Prompt Puzzle Masters: Teach students to craft effective AI prompts by breaking down complex questions and using descriptive language.
    • Grades 6-8:
      • Creating a Personal Song Using Suno AI: Explore AI art and music and develop critical thinking skills to evaluate AI-generated content.
      • Which Picture Is AI-Generated?: Understand AI-generated images and apply critical thinking to evaluate their authenticity.
      • Mastering the Art of Prompting: Harnessing the Power of AI: Learn to craft multi-stage AI prompts and consider ethical implications.
      • Unleash Your AI Superpowers: Combining Tools to Tackle Complex Challenges: Discover the power of combining AI tools to tackle complex problems.
      • Responsible AI: Navigating the Ethical Landscape: Explore AI ethics, fairness, and bias identification.
      • Navigating the AI Landscape: Governance, Privacy, and Safety: Discuss AI governance, privacy, and safety.
      • Unveiling the Impacts of AI: Societal, Environmental, and Creative Frontiers: Evaluate the societal, environmental, and creative impacts of AI.
    • Grades 9-12:
      • Decoding Biases in AI Image Generation: Explore the capabilities and limitations of generative AI in image generation and understand biases.

Please organize the alignment in tables by TCEA ELE for AI in Education. Use the exact language provided in the AI Literacy Framework for Students and include only the lesson titles in the 'Lessons from the AI Literacy Framework' column."
