Purpose Statement

This online, self-paced course titled "Google's AI Classroom" is designed for K-12 educators seeking to integrate Google's AI tools into their teaching practices. The course aims to equip educators with the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively use Google Gemini and Duet AI, enhancing both classroom instruction and administrative tasks. By the end of this course, participants will be able to leverage AI technologies to create more engaging, personalized, and efficient learning experiences for their students.

Course Module Overview

Module 1: Introduction to AI in Education: In this module, you will explore the basic concepts of AI and its potential in education. You'll gain an understanding of Google's AI tools and develop AI literacy essential for educators.

Module 2: Getting Started with Google Gemini: You will learn how to access and utilize Google Gemini effectively for classroom applications. This module covers basic functionalities and provides hands-on practice with the tool.

Module 3: Duet AI in Google Workspace: Explore how Duet AI integrates into Google Workspace to enhance productivity. You'll learn to use AI features in Google Docs, Slides, and Sheets for various educational tasks.

Module 4: Advanced AI Techniques for Classroom Enhancement: Discover advanced strategies for incorporating AI into your teaching practices. This module covers personalized learning, AI-assisted assessment, and enhancing student engagement using AI tools.

Module 5: Responsible AI Use and Future Trends: Understand the ethical implications of AI in education and stay updated with future trends. You'll learn about responsible AI use and how to prepare students for an AI-integrated future.
