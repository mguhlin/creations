Here's a table with activities aligned to each indicator for the Essential Learning Expectations for AI in Education, organized as requested:

1.0 AI Literacy

IndicatorActivityEducational Standard Correlation
1.1 I know how AI systems work and their capabilities in educational contexts.Students participate in a workshop where they build a simple machine learning model to predict test scores based on study time, discussing the model's strengths and limitations.ISTE Standard 5a: Students formulate problem definitions suited for technology-assisted methods such as data analysis, abstract models and algorithmic thinking in exploring and finding solutions.
1.2 I can explain AI concepts in simple terms to various stakeholders.Teachers create infographics explaining how AI is used in personalized learning platforms, presenting these to parents at a school open house.ISTE Standard 7c: Educators use collaborative tools to expand students' authentic, real-world learning experiences by engaging virtually with experts, teams, and students, locally and globally.
1.3 I am aware of the current limitations and potential biases in AI systems.Students analyze the results of facial recognition software across different demographics, discussing potential biases and their implications.ISTE Standard 3d: Students build knowledge by actively exploring real-world issues and problems, developing ideas and theories and pursuing answers and solutions.

2.0 Ethical AI Use

IndicatorActivityEducational Standard Correlation
2.1 I know how to apply ethical principles when using or developing AI for education.Administrators develop an AI ethics policy for the school district, outlining guidelines for data privacy, fairness, and transparency.ISTE Standard 2b: Educators advocate for equitable access to educational technology, digital content and learning opportunities to meet the diverse needs of all students.
2.2 I can identify potential ethical issues in AI applications and propose solutions.Students review case studies of AI applications in education, identifying potential ethical concerns and proposing mitigation strategies.ISTE Standard 7b: Students use collaborative technologies to work with others, including peers, experts or community members, to examine issues and problems from multiple viewpoints.
2.3 I am aware of the importance of transparency and accountability in AI systems.Teachers create a rubric for evaluating the transparency and accountability of AI tools before implementing them in their classrooms.ISTE Standard 3d: Students build knowledge by actively exploring real-world issues and problems, developing ideas and theories and pursuing answers and solutions.

3.0 AI-Enhanced Learning

IndicatorActivityEducational Standard Correlation
3.1 I know how to leverage AI tools to personalize and enhance learning experiences.Teachers use an AI-powered adaptive learning platform to create personalized math practice sessions for students.ISTE Standard 5b: Students collect data or identify relevant data sets, use digital tools to analyze them, and represent data in various ways to facilitate problem-solving and decision-making.
3.2 I can evaluate the effectiveness of AI-enhanced learning interventions.Instructional coaches conduct a study comparing student outcomes between classes using AI-enhanced learning tools and those using traditional methods.ISTE Standard 7a: Students use digital tools to connect with learners from a variety of backgrounds and cultures, engaging with them in ways that broaden mutual understanding and learning.
3.3 I am aware of how AI can support diverse learning needs and styles.Special education teachers explore AI-powered text-to-speech and speech-to-text tools to support students with learning disabilities.ISTE Standard 4d: Students exhibit a tolerance for ambiguity, perseverance and the capacity to work with open-ended problems.

4.0 AI and Critical Thinking

IndicatorActivityEducational Standard Correlation
4.1 I know how to promote critical thinking skills in relation to AI-generated information.Students compare AI-generated essays with human-written ones, analyzing differences in style, content, and accuracy.ISTE Standard 3b: Students evaluate the accuracy, perspective, credibility and relevance of information, media, data or other resources.
4.2 I can teach others to question and verify AI outputs.Teachers lead a workshop for colleagues on fact-checking AI-generated content using multiple sources.ISTE Standard 3c: Students curate information from digital resources using a variety of tools and methods to create collections of artifacts that demonstrate meaningful connections or conclusions.
4.3 I am aware of strategies to develop AI-related problem-solving skills.Students participate in an AI ethics hackathon, developing solutions to real-world AI challenges in education.ISTE Standard 4a: Students know and use a deliberate design process for generating ideas, testing theories, creating innovative artifacts or solving authentic problems.
