Course Overview

This online, self-paced course on Reciprocal Teaching is designed for K-12 educators seeking to enhance their instructional strategies and improve student reading comprehension. The course explores the theoretical foundations, practical applications, and implementation of Reciprocal Teaching across various educational contexts. By the end of this course, educators will be equipped with the knowledge and skills to effectively integrate Reciprocal Teaching into their classrooms, supporting diverse learners and promoting active engagement with texts.

Course Module Overview

Module 1: Foundations of Reciprocal Teaching

In this module, you will explore the theoretical underpinnings of Reciprocal Teaching and its significance in education. You'll examine John Hattie's research on visible learning and understand how Reciprocal Teaching fits into evidence-based instructional practices.

Module 2: Understanding Reading Challenges

You will delve into the common reading difficulties students face across grade levels and content areas. This module will help you identify various reading challenges and understand how Reciprocal Teaching can address these issues.

Module 3: The Four Strategies of Reciprocal Teaching

In this module, you will learn about the core components of Reciprocal Teaching: predicting, questioning, clarifying, and summarizing. You'll explore each strategy in depth and understand how they work together to improve reading comprehension.

Module 4: Implementing Reciprocal Teaching Across the Curriculum

You will discover how to apply Reciprocal Teaching across different subject areas and grade levels. This module focuses on adapting the strategy for various disciplines and addressing potential implementation challenges.

Module 5: Assessing and Enhancing Reciprocal Teaching Practices

In this final module, you will learn how to evaluate the effectiveness of Reciprocal Teaching in your classroom. You'll explore assessment strategies, technology integration, and methods for continuous improvement of your Reciprocal Teaching practice.

Module 1: Foundations of Reciprocal Teaching

1.0 About

"Reciprocal teaching refers to an instructional activity in which students become the teacher in small group reading sessions. Teachers model, then help students learn to guide group discussions using four strategies: summarizing, question generating, clarifying, and predicting. Once students have learned the strategies, they take turns assuming the role of teacher in leading a dialogue about what has been read." - Palincsar, A. S., & Brown, A. L. (1984)

Reciprocal Teaching is a powerful strategy that empowers students to take control of their learning. In this module, we'll explore its foundations and the research that supports its effectiveness. Let's dive in and discover how this approach can transform your classroom!
