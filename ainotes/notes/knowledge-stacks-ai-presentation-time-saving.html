Tip #1

Refer to your session description several times to ensure that you are planning to do what you said you would do.

Tip #2

Encourage active participation: Consider how you can get your attendees to talk and apply what they are learning before they leave your session.

Tip #3: Engagement Arsenal

1. Digital Organization

Harness the power of Mentimeter to create interactive word clouds. Watch as your students' ideas come to life in real-time!

2. Brain-Based Learning

Implement activities that align with how our brains process information. Boost retention and engagement simultaneously.

3. High-Effect Strategies

Utilize advance organizers to set the stage for learning. Give students a roadmap for the content ahead.

---

Tip #4: Use Digital Dynamos

  • Google Sites: Create a centralized hub for your content. Enable easy navigation and seamless collaboration with Google integration.
  • Canva Slide Deck: Design visually stunning slides with professional templates. Manage your presentation remotely and gather real-time feedback.
  • Loom Tutorials: Craft quick, engaging video tutorials. Show students step-by-step processes for using these powerful digital tools.

Tip #5: Use Brain Boosters

1. Inner-Outer Circle

Foster active listening and deep discussions. Students rotate between inner and outer circles, sharing diverse perspectives.

2. Paper Notecards

Boost creativity and retention through tactile brainstorming. Students jot down ideas and provide feedback on physical cards.

3. Digital Collaboration

Utilize Padlet for virtual brainstorming sessions. Create a digital wall of ideas accessible to all students.

---

Tip #6: Learning Levers

  • Jigsaw Reading: Divide content into sections. Students become experts and teach their peers, fostering collaboration and deep understanding.
  • Think-Pair-Share: Encourage individual reflection, pair discussion, and group sharing. Promote critical thinking and active participation.
  • Kahoot! Checks: Implement quick knowledge checks throughout your presentation. Gamify learning and reinforce key concepts with fun quizzes.

---

Tools to Promote Interactivity

  • Padlet: Use for digital brainstorming and collaborative idea generation.
  • Mentimeter: Gather real-time responses from attendees.
  • Google Forms: Ask attendees to submit ideas of how they could/would/will implement/apply what they are learning in your session. Then, share the Google Sheet with them as a resource of ideas.
  • Wheel of Names: Wheel of Names

// TCEA Style Guide

Avoid the Following:
  • Ampersands (&); instead, use "and."
  • Double spaces after a period.
  • Overuse of exclamation marks (!).
  • Displaying full hyperlinks; hyperlink the text instead.
  • Ending bulleted lists with periods unless the items are complete sentences.
Do the Following:
  • Use the Oxford comma.
  • Capitalize product names appropriately.
  • Hyperlink the text, not the URL.
  • Explain abbreviations (e.g., LMS, IEP).
  • Write out numbers up to ten.
  • Capitalize the first letter of each bulleted list item.
  • Place punctuation inside quotation marks when necessary.
  • Avoid commonly used words such as embark, empower, journey, delve, dive, discover, unlock, ensure, Dive into,tapestry, vibrant, landscape, realm, moreover, navigate, arguably, as well as phrases such as It's important to note…, Important to consider…, Based on the information provided…, Remember that…, Navigating the [landscape]/[complexities of], A testament to…, As an AI language model… , As of my last…
