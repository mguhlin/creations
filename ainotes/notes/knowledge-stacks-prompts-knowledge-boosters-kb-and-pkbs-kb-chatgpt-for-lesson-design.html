Description:

Transform your lesson planning process with ChatGPT as your intelligent teaching assistant. This comprehensive Knowledge Booster series equips K-16 educators with practical skills to leverage ChatGPT's latest capabilities for creating engaging, standards-aligned lessons in a fraction of the traditional time. Through hands-on practice with real classroom scenarios, you'll master prompt engineering techniques, explore ChatGPT's advanced features like Canvas and memory functions, and develop a sustainable workflow for AI-enhanced instruction.

What You'll Gain:

  • Master the P-T-C-F (Persona-Task-Context-Format) prompting framework for consistent, high-quality outputs
  • Create complete lesson plans, differentiated activities, and assessments in minutes instead of hours
  • Leverage ChatGPT's Canvas feature for collaborative editing and iterative refinement
  • Build a personal prompt library and implement responsible AI practices
  • Design inclusive, culturally responsive lessons with built-in differentiation strategies

Audience:

K-16 educators, instructional coaches, curriculum specialists, and educational technology coordinators seeking to enhance their lesson design efficiency while maintaining pedagogical excellence.

Prior Experience Needed:

No prior AI experience required. Basic computer skills and internet access recommended.

What the Series Includes:

This three-part series covers foundational prompt engineering, advanced ChatGPT features including Canvas and Projects, and implementation strategies for sustainable AI integration. Participants will create a complete mini-lesson, develop a reusable prompt library, and establish ethical guidelines for classroom AI use.

"Incorporate ChatGPT's newest features including Canvas for collaborative editing, Projects for curriculum organization, and memory functions for personalized assistance. Focus on practical classroom applications that save teachers time while improving differentiation."
