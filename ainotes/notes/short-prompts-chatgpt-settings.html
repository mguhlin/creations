If ChatGPT is not responding how you're telling it to, no matter how you say it, here's what may be happening (and how to fix it fast) 👇

ChatGPT (especially GPT-5) has a hard time with conflicting instructions if it is not explicitly instructed what to prioritize.

And there are several places you may be giving it conflicting instructions (many you may not even realize are there). Here are the top SIX places you may need to check:

1️⃣. Your prompt. Obvious, but worth checking first. Did you give conflicting or unclear directions? It’s easy to accidentally contradict yourself in a long prompt. It happens more often than you think.

2️⃣ Earlier in the conversation. For ChatGPT, anything you said earlier - or even something it said earlier - becomes part of what is essentially a “mega prompt” that can influence all of the later outputs in the conversation. If there’s a contradiction buried earlier, it may still be prioritizing it.

3️⃣. Your account level Custom Instructions. These act like a hidden, ever-present prompt that influences almost every chat. Go to Settings > Personalization and review what you’ve written there. Conflicts here can easily override what you say in a single prompt.

4️⃣. Your Memory. If Memory is turned on, ChatGPT uses what it remembers about you to shape its responses. That can help or hurt, depending on the context. For example, if it remembers you love em dashes, it might keep adding them — even when you don’t want them. Check what it’s remembering under Settings > Personalization > Memory > click the "Manage" Button.

5️⃣. Your Project-level Instructions. If you’re working inside a Project, those saved instructions can influence responses in the Project too. Click the three dots in the top right of your Project and check what’s in "Add Instructions."

6️⃣. Your GPT instructions. If you’re using a custom GPT, the behavior might be controlled by its built-in instructions. If it’s yours, open the editor and review them. If it’s someone else’s GPT, there’s not much you can change - you’ll need to work within their setup.

The bottom line is ChatGPT doesn’t always know which instructions to prioritize when multiple sources give it mixed signals. A lot of times cleaning up the potential conflict fixes the behavior, instantly.
