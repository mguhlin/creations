It’s fun that you asked because I was taking a project management certification course when I decided I was going to use those principals to write the book. It’s called a “book project” for a reason.

I “assigned” Claude the role of project manager, and I took the role as writer and scrum master.

Using this framework, I outlined the book and had Claude perform the research. After coming up with a proper outline, Claude and I got to work on each chapter.

We made a rough draft, and then I assigned Claude as the editor. After the editing suggestions, I made the appropriate notes and then we started making appropriate edits together.

After I was satisfied with the second draft, I assigned Claude as an “acquisition editor” of a publishing house, and had it evaluate and rate the book. It scored a high but not top-tier rating.

I took the recommendations to craft a third draft, and repeated the steps.

In the end, I had a really good manuscript with a high-percentage chance of it being accepted, which it was.
