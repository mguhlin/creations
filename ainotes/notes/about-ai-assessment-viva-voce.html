the key to an oral report is to hear what students can actually demonstrate themselves.

When we tolerate them reading huge chucks of text without looking up, it’s likely they are just reading AI generated text.

Poor communication & poor understanding.

This rubric line helped my students know what to aim for:

(D Grade) - Student consistently relies on notes as a script (paper or screen) to communicate information.

(C Grade) - Student reads off notes throughout presentation, occasionally addressing the audience directly.

(B Grade) - Student quickly refers to notes throughout the presentation as a reminder of key points or details.

(A Grade) - Student occasionally refers to notes as a reminder of key points or details.
