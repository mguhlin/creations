I am curious how others are using AI in e-Learning and in training development. I have been toying with an idea for how to make our content last longer. As someone who has always been a curator of content, I have spent years recognizing patterns across different videos and weaving them into cohesive narratives. One of the biggest challenges with video content is how to make it more evergreen. How do we make sure that our training materials, marketing assets, and instructional videos remain relevant over time?

I believe this is where AI can come in to help. Here is what I have started trying:

  1. Extract and Analyze Transcripts: I start by pulling the VTT transcripts from existing videos, removing the timing markers to focus on the raw content. I then use NotebookLM and Perplexity to store multiple transcripts and analyze them for common themes and connectors. I like both of these because they provide citations within results connected to the original source files.
  1. Construct a Unified Narrative: Once I identify the key takeaways and overlapping themes, I can pull relevant script clips into a new video framework script. AI helps me scaffold the material into a structured and cohesive messaging and training experience.
  1. Enhancing with AI Voiceovers: To maintain consistency and modernize the content, I use an AI-powered text-to-speech tool to deliver a refined, engaging vocal perspective to tie it all together.
  1. Updating Visuals & Design: Once the messaging and narration is in place, I can incorporate fresh graphics and animations to align with current branding.
  1. Multipurpose Deployment: The final video is not just used in our LMS for internal training but then it is also clipped and shared with marketing to amplify messaging for prospects and customers.

This process will allow me to take existing content, refresh it with a unifying voice, and extend its lifespan across multiple channels. Rather than recreating content from scratch, AI helps me refine and adapt it to make it more relevant, engaging and useful over time.

Anyone else trying a similar process? Or do you have a different process to make your content last longer? What AI tools are you using?
