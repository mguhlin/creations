SOLO StageActivityAI SupportAI Limitation StrategyCommunity Building Strategy
PrestructuralUse quizzes or brainstorming activities to gauge understanding of a writing genre or topic.AI provides only basic vocabulary lists or writing prompts to spark student thinking.Limit AI use to non-essential tasks like generating lists; students must explain connections to the topic.Sharing Circle: Students share initial ideas and prompts, offering feedback on their personal connections.
UnistructuralDraft single-sentence thesis statements or summaries.No AI use at this stage. Students craft statements independently, relying on teacher feedback or peer reviews.Emphasize manual revision through peer editing or rubric-based self-assessment to strengthen critical thinking.Talk Read, Talk Write: Students discuss readings in pairs or small groups before independently drafting thesis statements.
MultistructuralExplore multiple perspectives or collect supporting evidence for an argument.No AI use at this stage. Students research and organize sources manually to develop research skills and critical evaluation.Require students to analyze and summarize sources in their own words, supported by detailed teacher guidance.Sharing Circle: Writers share sources they’ve found, discuss strengths/weaknesses, and justify their inclusion in writing.
RelationalDevelop cohesive essays connecting multiple arguments.AI provides structural suggestions (e.g., outlining) after students create a full draft.Students must construct all connections, arguments, and transitions independently before using AI tools.Talk Read, Talk Write: Writers share their essay outlines with a peer group for feedback on argument cohesion before drafting.
Extended AbstractApply writing to novel scenarios, such as drafting policy recommendations or innovative solutions.Use AI as a brainstorming partner for generating counterarguments or refining ideas, only after a complete student draft.Students present a finalized draft for peer or teacher feedback first, limiting AI to final refinements only.Sharing Circle: Writers present their solutions, get peer questions, and refine their approach collaboratively before finalizing.
