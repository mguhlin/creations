What is an Agent?

  • People often use the term agent to describe any automated process involving AI
  • Technical definition:
    • an automation with some autonomy over how it accomplishes tasks.
  • A workflow is an automation with predefined tasks to accomplish a task.
  • An agent has autonomy and can make decisions on its own. They can use a set of tools to achieve the goal. They can also reflect on their progress.

General Workflow

1- Goal: What am I trying to do?
