Knowledge Booster: Reciprocal Teaching for K-16 Educators

Description:

This Knowledge Booster series equips K-16 educators with the tools and strategies to implement Reciprocal Teaching, a research-backed instructional approach with an impressive 0.74 effect size (Hattie). Through three comprehensive parts, you'll master the four essential strategies—predicting, questioning, clarifying, and summarizing—while learning to adapt them across grade levels and subject areas.

What You'll Gain:

• Master the four core Reciprocal Teaching strategies and their implementation

• Develop skills to address diverse reading challenges across content areas

• Learn to integrate technology and assessment tools for enhanced effectiveness

• Create differentiated approaches for multilingual learners and students with special needs

• Build a sustainable implementation plan with measurable outcomes

Audience:

K-16 educators, instructional coaches, reading specialists, and content-area teachers seeking evidence-based strategies to improve student reading comprehension and critical thinking skills.

Prior Experience Needed:

No prior experience with Reciprocal Teaching required. Basic classroom teaching experience helpful.

What the Series Includes:

Three comprehensive video sessions covering foundations and research base, practical implementation strategies, and assessment techniques, plus hands-on activities, ready-to-use templates, and transfer learning exercises designed to move knowledge into long-term memory.

Total Time Breakdown:

  • Part 1: 65 minutes (35 video + 15 hands-on + 15 transfer)
  • Part 2: 70 minutes (40 video + 15 hands-on + 15 transfer)
  • Part 3: 65 minutes (35 video + 15 hands-on + 15 transfer)
  • Total: 200 minutes = 2 CPE credits

Part 1: Foundations and the Power of the Essential Four

In this foundational part, we explore the research base behind Reciprocal Teaching, including Hattie's impressive 0.74 effect size, and introduce the four essential strategies. You'll learn why this approach works across disciplines and how to begin implementing these evidence-based strategies in your classroom immediately.
