How I Created a Classroom Seating Chart Free with ChatGPT (Instructions)

Hi teachers. I wanted to share a method I used to create a seating chart for my classroom using ChatGPT. It was easy and efficient.

Here’s how I did it:

  1. PREPARE YOUR STUDENT LIST

First, I prepared a list of my students' names in a simple text file. For privacy, I used first names only. Here’s an example of what my list looked like:

Alice, Bob, Charlie, Dana, Eve, Frank, Grace, Hank, Ivy, Jack, Luna, Mason, Nina, Oscar, Paul, Quincy, Rachel, Sam, Tina, Uma

  1. DESCRIBE YOUR CLASSROOM LAYOUT

Next, I provided ChatGPT with details about my classroom setup, such as the number of rows and columns, and any special seating requirements. This is what my description included:

Classroom Layout:

• Rows: 5

• Columns: 4

Special Seating Requirements:

  1. Alice needs to sit near the front due to vision issues.
  1. Bob and Charlie work well together, place them in the same row.
  1. Dana needs a quiet corner due to concentration difficulties.
  1. Eve and Frank should not be seated next to each other.
  1. Grace requires a seat close to the door for easy access.
  1. Hank prefers to sit in the back.
  1. INPUT STUDENT PREFERENCES

I made sure to include any specific seating preferences, like group work or special needs, to ensure the seating arrangement catered to everyone’s needs.

  1. GENERATE THE SEATING CHART

I then asked ChatGPT to create a seating chart based on the provided information and the student names. My request looked something like this:

Example Prompt:

"ChatGPT, please arrange my students in a 5x4 seating chart considering the following preferences:

Classroom Layout: Rows: 5, Columns: 4

Special Seating Requirements:

  1. Alice needs to sit near the front due to vision issues.
  1. Bob and Charlie work well together, place them in the same row.
  1. Dana needs a quiet corner due to concentration difficulties.
  1. Eve and Frank should not be seated next to each other.
  1. Grace requires a seat close to the door for easy access.
  1. Hank prefers to sit in the back." + the attached file.
  1. REVIEW AND ADJUST

After receiving the seating chart from ChatGPT, I reviewed it to ensure it fit my needs. I also had the option to export the final seating chart as a PDF or Excel document for easy access and sharing.

  1. CUSTOM GPT FOR DOCUMENT UPLOADS

If you’re looking to automate this even further, you can create a custom GPT in ChatGPT (or Claude) with specific instructions on what to do with an uploaded document.

Methods like this have saved me so much time and hassle, and I hope it helps you too!
