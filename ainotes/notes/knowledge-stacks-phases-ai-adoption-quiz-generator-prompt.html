Create a concise self-assessment quiz to help organizations determine their current phase of AI adoption. The quiz should:

  1. Consist of 5-7 multiple-choice questions.
  1. Cover key areas of AI adoption, including but not limited to:
    • AI strategy and policy
    • Resource allocation for AI
    • AI best practices and training
    • Integration of AI into operations
    • Community engagement and communication about AI
  1. Have 5 answer options for each question, representing a spectrum from early/basic adoption to advanced/leadership in AI use.
  1. Include a simple scoring system where each answer choice corresponds to a point value (e.g., 1-5 points).
  1. Provide a brief interpretation guide for the total score, categorizing respondents into different phases of AI adoption (e.g., Awareness, Exploration, Integration, Optimization, Leadership).
  1. Be written in clear, jargon-free language accessible to individuals with varying levels of AI knowledge.
  1. Be designed to be completed in about 5-10 minutes.
  1. Include a brief set of instructions for taking the self-assessment.

Please present the quiz in a format that's easy to implement in a digital survey tool or as a printable document. Also, provide a short paragraph explaining how organizations can use the results of this self-assessment to inform their AI strategy and identify areas for improvement.

AI Adoption Self-Assessment Quiz

Instructions

For each question in the table below, select the option (A to E) that most accurately reflects your organization's current AI adoption status. Each option corresponds to a point value (A=1, B=2, C=3, D=4, E=5). After completing the quiz, sum up your points from all five questions. Your total score will indicate your organization's primary AI adoption phase: 5-9 (AI Secured), 10-14 (AI Exploration), 15-19 (AI Pilot Programs), 20-24 (AI Unification), or 25 (AI Everywhere). Use your results to evaluate your organization's AI adoption progress and identify areas for improvement.

Scoring and Interpretation

Total your points from all questions and refer to the following guide:

  • 5-9 points: AI Secured Phase - Your organization is in the early stages of AI adoption, focusing primarily on security and risk management.
  • 10-14 points: AI Exploration Phase - You're beginning to explore AI possibilities and experimenting with limited applications.
  • 15-19 points: AI Pilot Programs Phase - Your organization is implementing targeted AI programs and developing more comprehensive strategies.
  • 20-24 points: AI Unification Phase - AI is becoming unified across your organization, with standardized practices and widespread integration.
  • 25 points: AI Everywhere Phase - Your organization is a leader in AI adoption, with AI fully integrated into all aspects of operations and strategy.

Use these results to identify areas for improvement and inform your AI strategy going forward. Focus on advancing in areas where you scored lower to move to the next phase of AI adoption.
