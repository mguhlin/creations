Course Intro

The purpose of this self-paced, online course is to empower K-12 educators with the knowledge and skills to effectively integrate Perplexity AI into their teaching practices. As artificial intelligence continues to reshape the educational landscape, it's crucial for educators to stay ahead of the curve and harness the potential of AI tools to enhance student learning.

Perplexity AI offers a unique opportunity to revolutionize research, lesson planning, and administrative tasks in education. By leveraging this powerful tool, educators can foster critical thinking, personalize learning experiences, and streamline their workflow. According to a recent study by the World Economic Forum, AI in education is expected to grow by 47.5% between 2017 and 2021, making it essential for educators to develop AI literacy.

This course will guide participants through the fundamentals of Perplexity AI, its applications in various subject areas, and advanced features for educational use. By the end of this course, educators will be equipped to:

  1. Utilize Perplexity AI for efficient research and lesson planning
  1. Integrate AI-enhanced learning activities across different subjects
  1. Address diverse learning needs through personalized content creation
  1. Streamline administrative tasks and improve communication
  1. Stay current with emerging AI trends in education

Participants will gain hands-on experience with Perplexity AI, access ready-made templates and resources, and collaborate with peers to explore innovative ways of implementing AI in their classrooms. This course is designed to build confidence in using AI tools and to inspire educators to become leaders in AI-enhanced education.

Intended Audience

This course is designed for K-16 educators, including teachers, administrators, librarians, and instructional coaches. It caters to professionals working in elementary, middle, and high schools, as well as those in higher education settings. The content is tailored to help educators at all levels integrate Perplexity AI into their teaching practices, research methods, and administrative tasks, regardless of their prior experience with AI tools.

Course Module Overview

Module 1: Discover Perplexity

You will explore Perplexity AI's core functions and its unique position in the AI landscape. You'll learn about key features like the Library, Collections, Threads, and Pages. You'll set up your account, navigate the interface, and consider the ethical implications of AI in education.

Module 2: Research Smarter

You will hone your skills in using Perplexity AI for research and information management. You'll master advanced query techniques, organize information effectively, and learn to generate Python code snippets for basic data analysis.

Module 3: Transform Teaching

You will explore practical applications of Perplexity AI in the classroom. You'll integrate AI across subjects, create comprehensive resources, and develop AI-enhanced learning activities. You'll also guide students in responsible AI use for their research and learning.

Module 4: Streamline Administration

You will learn to use Perplexity AI for administrative and collaborative work. You'll analyze educational data, generate reports, facilitate team projects, and enhance professional development. You'll also create effective data visualizations to present information.

Module 5: Innovate Education

You will examine advanced applications of Perplexity AI and its future in education. You'll integrate Perplexity AI with other educational technologies, explore complex research tasks, and stay updated with new AI developments. You'll also create an action plan for implementing Perplexity AI in your educational setting.
