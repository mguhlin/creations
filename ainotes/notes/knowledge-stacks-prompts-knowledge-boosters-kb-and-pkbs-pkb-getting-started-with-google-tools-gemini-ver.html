Description

You have probably seen new icons and "Gemini" features popping up in your Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. What is this new Artificial Intelligence (AI), and how is it supposed to help you or your child? It can feel confusing, but these new tools offer incredible opportunities to support your child's learning, get organized, and even make homework more creative.

This 30-minute Parent Knowledge Booster is designed to demystify Google's Generative AI. We will skip the basics of "how to open a Doc" and focus on the new AI-powered features. You will learn what these tools can (and cannot) do, how to use them as a "helper" for your family, and, most importantly, how to have conversations with your children about using them safely and responsibly.

What You'll Gain

  • Understand what Google's AI (Gemini) is and where to find it in the tools you already use
    • Learn how to use AI as a "writing partner" in Google Docs to brainstorm ideas, draft emails, or summarize long documents
    • Discover how AI in Google Sheets and Keep can help you and your child organize projects, track deadlines, and manage tasks
    • Explore "NotebookLM," a powerful and safe AI research tool that helps your child study from their own class notes and materials
    • Feel more confident talking to your child about using AI tools ethically and responsibly for school

Who This Is For

This series is for parents and guardians of students of all ages (from elementary through college) who have a basic familiarity with Google Tools but want to understand and leverage the new, free GenerAI features to support their families.

What You Need to Get Started

  • A free Google account
    • Access to a computer or smartphone with an internet connection
    • An open mind and a willingness to explore\!

What the Series Includes

In just 30 minutes, we will take a tour of Google's new AI landscape. Part 1 introduces you to your new AI assistant, "Gemini," and shows you how it works inside Google Docs and Drive. Part 2 focuses on using AI to get organized, helping you and your child tame the chaos of schedules and projects with Google Sheets and Keep. Finally, Part 3 dives into using AI for creative projects in Slides and introduces NotebookLM, a game-changing (and safe) tool for research and studying.

Total Time Breakdown:

  • Part 1: 10 minutes (6 min video + 2 min hands-on + 2 min reflection)
    • Part 2: 10 minutes (6 min video + 2 min hands-on + 2 min reflection)
    • Part 3: 10 minutes (6 min video + 2 min hands-on + 2 min reflection)
    • Total: 30 minutes

Part 1: Your New AI Assistant: Understanding Gemini in Drive and Docs

In this first part, we will meet "Gemini," Google's new AI helper that's built right into Docs and Drive. We will explore what it is, why it matters for you, and how you can use it as a "writing partner" to brainstorm ideas or as a "librarian" to summarize long documents. This skill helps you model responsible AI use and support your child when they feel "stuck" on a writing assignment.

Hands-On Activity (2 minutes)

Take 2 minutes to try this right now.

  1. Open a new, blank Google Doc.
  1. Find the "Help me write" button (it may look like a sparkle icon or a pen in a box).
  1. In the prompt box, type: "Help me write a list of 5 study tips for a history test."
  1. Review what it creates. Now, try a different prompt: "Help me write a polite email to a teacher asking for a meeting about my child's progress."

By the end, you'll have two examples of AI-generated text and a feel for how the tool works.

Transfer Learning to Long-Term Memory (2 minutes)

Reflect: Think about one writing task your child struggles with (like starting an essay, writing an email, or taking notes). How could you introduce the "Help me write" tool as a helper or brainstorming partner, not a replacement for their own thinking? Jot down one to two sentences you could say to them.

AI can create images in Slides and translate in real-time in Meet, but its real power for students is in research. In this final part, we will introduce NotebookLM, a safe AI tool that helps students study from their own documents. We will also discuss how to talk to your child about using all these AI tools responsibly and avoiding plagiarism.

Hands-On Activity (2 minutes)

Take 2 minutes to explore this amazing tool.

  1. In your browser, go to notebooklmhttps://www.google.com/search?q=.google.com and log in with your Google account.
  1. Create a "New Notebook."
  1. Find a document on your computer to upload (like a PDF of a school handout, a recipe, or any article).
  1. Once it's uploaded, ask it a question in the chat box at the bottom, like: "What are the three main ideas from this document?" or "Create a five-question quiz based on this."

By the end, you'll see how NotebookLM gives you answers based only on the documents you provided.
