BYE-BYE SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGERS.

ChatGPT now does my 30 days of work in just 2 hours.

Here are 7 prompts to turn ChatGPT into your personal assistant for FREE:

⸻

  1. Full Social Media Strategy

“I want you to act as my personal social media strategist. Create a complete 30-day content strategy for my [insert niche]. Include post types, daily themes, hashtags, optimal posting times, and engagement techniques to grow followers and conversions, all tailored to my audience.”

⸻

  1. Viral Content Idea Generator

“Act as a viral content expert. Based on my niche [insert niche], generate 30 highly shareable and unique content ideas for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn that have high engagement potential. Each idea should have a title, hook, and call to action.”

⸻

  1. Viral Video Scriptwriter

“Act as a viral video scriptwriter. Write 5 short-form video scripts (30–60 seconds each) for [insert niche/product]. Each script should have a strong hook, storytelling flow, value point, and CTA that fits my platform (Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts).”

⸻

  1. Caption & Hashtag Wizard

“I want you to act as my creative caption and hashtag expert. Write 10 viral captions for my post about [insert topic], using emotional triggers, storytelling, and humor. Then, create 15 SEO-optimized hashtags that help me reach my ideal audience.”

⸻

  1. Social Media Analytics Interpreter

“I want you to act as my analytics expert. Based on this data [paste engagement metrics or analytics screenshot], tell me which posts performed best and why. Then, suggest how to replicate and improve that success over the next 30 days.”

⸻

  1. Social Media Ad Copy

“Act as my paid ads expert. Write 3 high-converting ad copies for [insert product/service] using AIDA and PAS frameworks. Each should have a hook, emotion-driven copy, and strong CTA for Facebook and Instagram campaigns.”

⸻

  1. Content Calendar Builder

“I want you to create a full monthly social media content calendar for my [insert niche]. Include content type, post caption, hashtags, posting time, and daily engagement task — formatted in a ready-to-use table.”
