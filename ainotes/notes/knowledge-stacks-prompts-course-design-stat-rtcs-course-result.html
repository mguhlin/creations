This course provides an overview of the recurring themes in the new Science TEKS (Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills) framework. You will explore key concepts such as structure and function, stability and change, energy and matter, systems and system models, and patterns and cause and effect. The course is designed to equip K-12 educators with the knowledge and strategies needed to integrate these themes into their science instruction effectively, fostering deeper understanding and engagement among students.

Course Module Overview with Titles and Descriptions

Module 1: Structure and Function

In this module, you will explore how the structure of objects and living things relates to their function. You'll examine how this relationship is foundational across scientific disciplines and learn how to apply these concepts to solve problems and analyze complex systems.

Module 2: Stability and Change

You will discover what factors contribute to the stability of systems and why systems change over time. This module will help you understand the role of feedback mechanisms in maintaining stability and how systems respond to changes and disturbances.

Module 3: Energy and Matter

Explore how energy and matter are conserved and transferred in different processes. In this module, you will learn about the different forms of energy, their transformations, and how energy and matter flow through both natural and designed systems.

Module 4: Systems and System Models

In this module, you will learn about systems thinking and the use of models in science and engineering. You'll explore the components and boundaries of systems, develop models, and analyze interactions within and between systems to gain a deeper understanding of their behavior.

Module 5: Patterns and Cause and Effect

This module focuses on how scientists identify and use patterns in nature to establish cause-and-effect relationships. You will learn how to recognize patterns, make predictions, and distinguish between correlation and causation in scientific phenomena.
