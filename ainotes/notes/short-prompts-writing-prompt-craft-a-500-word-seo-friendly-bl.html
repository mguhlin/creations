Prompt:

Craft a 500-word SEO-friendly blog post using the following guidelines.**

1. Context Transformation

Objective: Transform the provided educational context into a structured, education-focused blog post. Let me know when you are ready to receive it.

Steps:

  1. Key Points Analysis: Identify and develop 3-5 key points that are relevant to educators from the provided content.
  1. Structured Article Creation:
    • Introduction: Begin with an engaging question, a quote from an educator, or a brief classroom scenario.
      • Main Points: Present the key points using practical examples and classroom scenarios. Use H2 tags for main subheadings and H3 tags for any sub-points.
      • Conclusion: Summarize the main points in 2-3 sentences.
      • Guided Activities Table: Include a table that outlines activities educators can implement in their classrooms.
      • Additional Resources: List resources with titles featuring embedded bolded links (e.g., **source**), a brief description, and the target audience (K-12, higher education, etc.).
  1. Title and Description:
    • Catchy Title: Create a 2-3 word title that reflects the content's focus.
      • SEO-friendly Title: Ensure the title is SEO-friendly and consists of 2-3 words without colons.
      • Blog Description: Write a brief description of the blog content.
  1. Classroom Scenarios:
    • For each key point, write a 2-3 sentence scenario that illustrates how a teacher might apply the concept or tool in a classroom setting. Include realistic quotes from educators to make the scenarios relatable.
  1. Content Avoidance: Avoid overused words and phrases like "empower," "delve into," and "revolutionary." Do not include phrases like "It's important to note…" or "Based on the information provided…"
  1. TCEA Style Guide Compliance:
    • Use the Oxford comma.
      • Capitalize product names.
      • Hyperlink the text, not the link itself (e.g., **TCEA Courses**).
      • Write out numbers up to ten (e.g., "Take three of these…" instead of "Take 3 of these…").
  1. SEO Keyword: Provide one SEO keyword for the blog entry and label it at the bottom of the post.
  1. Additional Elements:
    • Feature Image Suggestion: Provide a description of what the feature image should look like.
      • Relevant Resources: Add titles with embedded links and short descriptions of other relevant TCEA blog entries.
      • Short Blurb: Write a brief blurb in the style of the examples provided below the blog entry.

2. Specific Instructions

  • Target Audience: Specify whether the blog is aimed at K-12 teachers, higher education instructors, or another group.
  • Desired Word Count: The target word count is 500 words.
  • Key Focus: Specify the main educational focus, such as classroom application or technology integration.

3. TCEA Style Guide

Avoid the Following:
  • Ampersands (&); instead, use "and."
  • Double spaces after a period.
  • Overuse of exclamation marks (!).
  • Displaying full hyperlinks; hyperlink the text instead.
  • Ending bulleted lists with periods unless the items are complete sentences.
Do the Following:
  • Use the Oxford comma.
  • Capitalize product names appropriately.
  • Hyperlink the text, not the URL.
  • Explain abbreviations (e.g., LMS, IEP).
  • Write out numbers up to ten.
  • Capitalize the first letter of each bulleted list item.
  • Place punctuation inside quotation marks when necessary.

Please let me know when you are ready to receive the educational content to make into a blog entry.
