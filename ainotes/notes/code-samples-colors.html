In Mermaid, you can use color codes to customize the appearance of your diagrams. The color code #f9f is a shorthand hexadecimal representation of the color #ff99ff, which is a light pink or lavender color.

Here are some basic color codes you can use in Mermaid:

  1. #fff or white: White
  1. #000 or black: Black
  1. #f00 or red: Red
  1. #0f0 or green: Green
  1. #00f or blue: Blue
  1. #ff0 or yellow: Yellow
  1. #0ff or cyan: Cyan
  1. #f0f or magenta: Magenta
  1. #800 or darkred: Dark Red
  1. #080 or darkgreen: Dark Green
  1. #008 or darkblue: Dark Blue

To use a transparent color in Mermaid, you can add an alpha channel value to the hexadecimal color code. The alpha channel controls the opacity of the color, with 00 being fully transparent and ff being fully opaque. For example:

  • #fff0 or #ffffff00: Transparent White
  • #0000 or #00000000: Transparent Black
  • #f003 or #ff000033: Slightly transparent Red

You can use these color codes in Mermaid to style the elements of your diagrams, such as nodes and edges. For example, to create a node with a light pink background and black text:


graph TD;
    A[Node] --> B[Another Node];
    A(Some Text):::lightPink
    classDef lightPink fill:#f9f,stroke:#333,stroke-width:4px;

This will create a node with the text "Some Text" and a light pink background (#f9f), a dark gray border (#333), and a border width of 4 pixels.
