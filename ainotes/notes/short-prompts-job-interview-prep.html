Then, based on the analysis, prepare a "grocery list"' of aspects of the Department and University that would be valuable to learn more about. Next, print your grocery list. Then, search the internet for information about the Department and the University and map that information on to the grocery list. Finally, put this information into a table and cite your sources, including links in a separate column on the table linking to an internet-based resources.
