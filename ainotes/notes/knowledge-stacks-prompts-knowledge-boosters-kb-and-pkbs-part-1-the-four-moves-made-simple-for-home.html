Part 1: The Four Moves—Made Simple for Home

We’ll turn “predict, question, clarify, summarize” into a quick conversation you can do with any text. This matters because short, steady talks build your child’s confidence and comprehension without adding stress. You’ll see how each move sounds in real life and practice with sentence starters.

Video

  • Watch Part 1 video (6 minutes) with slide deck (PDF)
  • Teaser: See exactly how a 2-minute “reading talk” sounds with an elementary and a middle school example.

Materials for You

  • One-Page Routine Card (4 Moves in 4 Minutes) – English & Spanish
  • Family Sentence Starters (bilingual) for each move
  • Quick Prompts Bookmark (printable or phone-sized PNG)
  • “Pick-a-Text” Ideas List for busy nights
  • Calm Coaching Phrases (what to say when your child resists)

Hands-On Activity (2 minutes)

Take 2 minutes to set up tonight’s routine. Open the One-Page Routine Card and choose one sentence starter for each move (predict, question, clarify, summarize). Write or screenshot your four picks. By the end, you’ll have a tiny script you can use with any text tonight.

Transfer Learning to Long-Term Memory (2 minutes)

Plan: Circle a real moment you’ll try this (bedtime story, homework passage, sports article). Jot down when and which four starters you’ll use. Add one way you’ll keep it positive (e.g., “I’ll praise the effort, not ‘right answers’.”).
