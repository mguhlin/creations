*Master Prompt for Style Analysis and Replication

Here is a complex, reusable "master prompt" designed to analyze a given text sample and generate a new, detailed prompt that can be used to replicate that style:

Target Text Analysis and Master Prompt Generation

Phase 1: Analyze the Text Sample

I will provide you with a piece of text. Your first task is to act as a linguistic analyst and a style expert to dissect the sample. Perform a deep analysis across the following dimensions:

  1. Sentence Structure & Rhythm:
    • Identify the dominant sentence lengths (e.g., short, punchy; long, complex).
      • Determine the average clause complexity (e.g., simple, compound, complex).
      • Note the prevalence of sentence types (e.g., declarative, rhetorical, fragmented).
      • Describe the overall reading rhythm (e.g., rapid and energetic, slow and deliberate).
  1. Vocabulary & Diction:
    • Identify a list of 10-15 key, characteristic words or phrases that define the text's vocabulary.
      • Determine the level of formality (e.g., highly formal, conversational, technical, academic).
      • Note the use of jargon, slang, or specialized terminology.
      • Identify the primary use of figurative language (e.g., metaphors, similes, hyperbole, alliteration).
  1. Tone & Voice:
    • Specify the primary tone (e.g., authoritative, humorous, critical, optimistic, urgent, detached).
      • Describe the underlying voice (e.g., first-person confessional, omniscient narrator, objective reporter, sales pitch).
      • Identify the intended emotional impact on the reader.
  1. Formatting & Presentation:
    • Note the use of paragraphing (e.g., short paragraphs for scannability, long blocks for density).
      • Observe the use of lists, headings, bolding, or other typographical features.
      • Identify any characteristic structural elements (e.g., a repeating sign-off, consistent use of a certain type of opener).

Phase 2: Generate the Master Prompt

Based on the detailed analysis in Phase 1, generate a single, highly detailed, and reusable "Master Prompt" that will instruct any AI model to generate new content in the exact same style.

The Master Prompt must be formatted as follows:

[MASTER PROMPT START]

ROLE: (The persona the AI must adopt, e.g., "An experienced financial analyst," "A witty, 19th-century essayist.")

TONE & VOICE: (Summary of Tone and Voice from Phase 1.)

STYLE GUIDELINES: (A bulleted list summarizing the key findings from Sentence Structure, Vocabulary, and Diction. This is the core instruction set.)

FORMATTING RULES: (A bulleted list of presentation and structural rules.)

TASK: (A placeholder that instructs the AI where to insert the new topic, e.g., "Write a [TYPE OF CONTENT, e.g., blog post, email, report] on the following topic: [INSERT NEW TOPIC HERE].")

[MASTER PROMPT END]

Execute:

Here is the text sample for analysis:

[INSERT YOUR TEXT SAMPLE HERE]
