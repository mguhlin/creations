Results

Here's the completed rubric for the Five Phases of District AI Adoption in K-12 public school districts:

CategoryDistrict PolicyAccount ManagementDistrict GuidanceCurriculum IntegrationCommunity Outreach

Blend into District Daily Use | AI tools fully integrated into district policies and procedures | Seamless single sign-on for all AI tools across the district | Comprehensive, regularly updated guidance on AI use for all stakeholders | AI tools embedded across curriculum and used daily by students and teachers | Ongoing community engagement and education on AI benefits and responsible use |

Standardize on AI Tools | District-wide policy on approved AI tools and usage guidelines | Centralized management of AI tool accounts for all users | Detailed guidance on using standardized AI tools effectively | AI tools integrated into specific subjects and grade levels | Regular updates to families on AI initiatives and student progress |

Deploy for Specific Purpose | Policy allowing AI use for targeted educational purposes | Managed accounts for specific AI tools as needed | Guidelines for using AI in designated areas (e.g. writing assistance, math practice) | AI tools introduced in select classes or for specific projects | Information sessions for parents on purpose and benefits of AI deployment |

Encourage Educator Experimentation | Policy permitting teacher exploration of AI tools | Individual teacher accounts for AI tools allowed | Basic guidance on evaluating and using AI tools responsibly | Teachers piloting AI tools in their classrooms | Informing parents about AI experimentation in classrooms |

Ban Digital Tools | Policy prohibiting use of AI tools | No accounts allowed for AI tools | Guidance on enforcing AI tool ban | No integration of AI tools in curriculum | Communicating AI ban to parents and community |

This rubric shows a progression from banning AI tools entirely to fully integrating them into daily district operations, with increasing levels of policy support, account management, guidance, curriculum integration, and community outreach at each stage.
