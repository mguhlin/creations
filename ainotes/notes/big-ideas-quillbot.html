ls:

1)

https://quillbot.com/ - for seeing alternative versions of my sentences. It is still my favorite after 2.5 years of use because its very fast (you don't have to provide a prompt), and you immediately get 7 different paraphrased versions of your sentence(s) in different styles (I have a paid version, but a free one is also great.)

2)

https://claude.ai/ - my go-to text editor and text summarizer. Usually I provide one or more sentences from my text and ask it to improve them, show me possible multiple versions. Or I may give it a paragraph and ask to add a sentence expressing a particular idea that stylistically and logically fits with the rest of the paragraph. Or I may write down in the prompt and ask it to turn it into a grammatical sentence.

More often than not, I have to keep asking over and over, pointing out that I do not like a particular word or phrase, until I get what I like.

Another function that works well for me is summarizing. I use it to draft summaries of my book chapters.

However, while editing my texts, in almost all cases I can't use Claude outputs fully as is—I find that I usually need to change something myself, remove redundancies, ask Claude to generate new versions, and so on. And this means that often AI bots don't really save me time, but paradoxically make the writing process paradoxically longer—because there is always a temptation to refine every paragraph further.

But its magical, wonderful, and amazing to have such a knowledgeable editor next to me all the time, regardless!

3)

https://www.perplexity.ai/ - I often use it instead of Google Search now. Like with all AI tools, the results can be hit or miss, but they contain multiple links to web sources, so you can follow these links and check the originals.

***

Bonus: for working with large numbers of text files, NotebookML is very good.
