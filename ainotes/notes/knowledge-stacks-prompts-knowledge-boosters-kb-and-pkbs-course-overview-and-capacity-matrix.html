Course Module Overview

Module 1: Foundations of Visual Learning with Diagrams.net

In this module, you will discover the power of visual learning and how a free tool like Diagrams.net can transform your instruction. You will learn the essential features of the interface, create and style your first diagrams, and understand the core principles of communicating ideas visually.

Module 2: Designing for the Classroom

In this module, you will move from basic shapes to creating powerful, curriculum-aligned learning aids. You will explore how to build common educational diagrams from scratch, including graphic organizers, flowcharts, and timelines, and learn to leverage pre-existing templates to save valuable time.

Module 3: Advanced Techniques and Customization

In this module, you will unlock the full potential of Diagrams.net by mastering its advanced features. You will learn to work with layers, create custom shape libraries for specific subjects, and apply sophisticated styling to produce professional-quality visuals for your lessons and presentations.

Module 4: Amplifying Learning with AI Integration

In this module, you will explore the cutting edge of diagramming by integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) into your workflow. You will learn how to use AI tools to automatically generate diagrams from text and outlines, and you will design AI-enhanced assignments that challenge students to think critically.

Module 5: Collaboration, Assessment, and Best Practices

In this module, you will focus on sharing your creations and using them to measure student understanding. You will learn how to embed diagrams into your Learning Management System (LMS), collaborate with colleagues on shared files, and use visual artifacts as a tool for formative assessment.
