Cartography & GIS Studio

A GIS (Geographic Information System) builds maps from stacked layers of data. In class we use a cardstock base with clear transparency sheets on top. Here, each layer is a transparent overlay you can draw on and toggle on/off — just like the real thing.

📋 Your scenario: A small island community needs a map. Base layer → draw the island, its trees & mountains. Disaster layer → draw a natural disaster striking the island. Solution layer → design a way the people could avoid or survive the disaster. Then finish your map with a title, scale, legend & compass.
