Place Builder

"Place" is how we describe what makes a location special — its name, its features (landforms, water, plants & animals), its environment (like weather), and its unique qualities. Read the story, hunt for the words that describe its place, then build a model map of what you pictured.

1The story

Click any underlined word and file it under the kind of place-detail it gives you.

Abuela's adobe house sat at the end of a dusty road in Mesa Verde, where the mesas rose flat and red against a bright afternoon sky. The warm wind carried the smell of cedar and far-off rain. A crooked screen door banged in the breeze, and on the cool back porch a roof of tin kept off the setting sun. Beyond the yard, a thin creek wound between cottonwood trees where jackrabbits darted through the dry brush. By night the desert turned cold, and coyotes called from the canyon. It was an old place, a cozy place — the only place that ever felt like home.
