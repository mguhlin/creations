Lava & Viscosity Lab

💡 Teacher / science notes

Warming a fluid lowers its viscosity (it flows faster). Adding water thins corn syrup (lower viscosity, faster). Adding solids like quinoa raises viscosity and adds friction (slower, and may flow unevenly — like a blocky lava). Steeper slopes increase the gravity pulling the flow downhill. Real basaltic lava is low-viscosity and fast; silica-rich rhyolite is high-viscosity and erupts explosively.
