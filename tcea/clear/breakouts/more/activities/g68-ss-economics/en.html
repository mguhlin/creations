🎡 Mission Brief

Mission: Open Before the Gates

You and your crew run the busiest funnel cake stand at the Texas State Fair. The owner, Mr. Ruiz, texted: "Cash box code is hidden in my booth notes. Figure it out so you can make change when the gates open at 10:00!" The notes are scattered evidence cards about prices, supplies, and sales. Read every clue carefully. Solve each lock using ONLY what the notes prove, not what you already know about fairs. One note is just doodles and will trick you if you let it. Reason it out and pop the box open.
