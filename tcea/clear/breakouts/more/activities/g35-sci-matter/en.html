🧊 Mission Brief

Mission: Reopen the Science Vault

You are a junior lab scientist at Ranger Elementary. Last night a storm knocked out the power and the freezer warmed up. To keep the samples safe, the lab's automatic vault locked itself shut. The only way to reopen it is to read the lab notebook (the CLUES) and answer the safety questions (the LOCKS). Warning: the vault will NOT accept guesses. Every answer must be proven by what the notebook actually says. Some pages are scribbles that don't help at all, so read carefully and ignore what doesn't matter. Open the clues, think like a scientist, and free the samples!
