🔬 Mission Brief

Mission: Identify Specimen X Before the Vault Reseals

It is 3:00 PM and the bio-vault is about to lock for the weekend with a single unknown specimen still inside. Dr. Reyes left you four evidence cards from her microscope notes but never wrote down what the cell actually is. Your team's job: use ONLY the evidence on the cards to decide whether Specimen X is a plant cell or an animal cell, prove which structures it must have, and explain how each part's shape matches its job. Solve the locks in order to open the vault. Warning: one of Dr. Reyes's cards is just a sticky note with nothing useful on it. Do not let it fool you.
