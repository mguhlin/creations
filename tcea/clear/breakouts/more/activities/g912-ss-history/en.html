🕵️ Mission Brief

Case File: Rebuild the Chain of Causes

You are a junior historian at the State Documents Vault. A major national conflict tore the country apart, and the public wants a simple answer: 'it just happened.' Your job is to prove otherwise. The vault holds four real evidence cards and one useless scrap that got filed by mistake. Using ONLY what these documents actually say, you must reconstruct the chain of cause and effect and place the events in the correct order — reasoning from the evidence, NOT from any date you happen to remember. Solve every lock to seal your findings into the official record and show that history builds, step by step, toward a breaking point.
