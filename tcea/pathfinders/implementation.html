Scenario for a Sample Project

Scenario

You are a school public relations expert. The District wants you to achieve the following objectives for the bond program:

  • Gain community support and approval for a K-12 school bond program aimed at improving school facilities and resources.
  • Implement the communications plan to raise awareness and support for the bond program.
  • Ensure all goals and outcomes of the public relations project have been achieved.
  • Reflect on the project’s execution and outcomes, and report to stakeholders.

Use the action phases below to begin your planning.

▸

Sample Project, Phase by Phase

START — Define what success looks like

Objective: Establish a clear vision of success for the bond program, focused on community support and engagement.

Action steps:

  • Define project goals, emphasizing improved school facilities and resources.
  • Identify and connect with key stakeholders: students, parents, staff, local businesses, and officials.
  • Gather data through community surveys, testimonials, and direct feedback to understand expectations and concerns.
  • Secure district approval and budget support for the project.

Deliverable: A proposal or executive summary outlining the project’s vision, goals, and initial stakeholder feedback.

PLAN — Align budget to goals and craft a timeline

Objective: Develop a detailed plan that aligns resources with goals and lays out a clear timeline.

Action steps:

  • Align the budget with defined goals, funding promotional materials, events, and advertising.
  • Craft a communications plan with key messages, channels, milestones, and responsibilities.
  • Identify major milestones and establish a timeline for execution.

Deliverable: A communications plan within the proposal, detailing strategy, timeline, and budget allocation.

EXECUTE — Implement the plan and monitor progress

Objective: Run the communications plan to raise awareness and support, with frequent updates and monitoring.

Action steps:

  • Make the plan accessible to all stakeholders and provide frequent progress updates.
  • Log progress on the project website, updating KPIs to track engagement and support.
  • Verify tasks and responsibilities so all team members stay on track.

Deliverable: A short email update on project steps, highlighting progress, challenges, and next steps.

STOP — Verify outcomes and reflect on execution

Objective: Ensure all goals and outcomes have been achieved, and reflect on successes and improvements.

Action steps:

  • Collect stakeholder feedback to verify deliverables and measure support for the bond program.
  • Create a summary report or video capturing stakeholder stories, successes, and lessons learned.
  • Reflect on execution: what worked well, what could improve, and how to carry it forward.

Deliverable: A verified set of outcomes and a reflection that informs future projects.
