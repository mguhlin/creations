Planning & Assessing Technology Integration

How do you gauge the success of blending technology into teaching and learning? This session explores multiple frameworks for assessing technology integration, each offering a different lens for understanding how to support student growth and impact achievement.

"What I'm starting to gain a greater appreciation for is that the best results are achieved not by talking about the how, but by talking about the why." — Julie Zhuo

What This Session Covers

Each framework here provides a different perspective for understanding how to facilitate student growth. Move through the five parts in order, or jump to the one you need.

1 · The Past

Where we have been with edtech, told as a timeline of milestones from wikis to artificial intelligence.

2 · The Classroom

Models that shape integration: project-based learning, constructivism, one-to-one, STEM, blended, and flipped.

3 · Research

What the evidence says about technology and achievement, and why purpose matters more than the tool.

4 · Perspectives

Frameworks and audits for observing and assessing technology use, from T3 to TPACK to SAMR.

5 · Tips

Practical moves for technology planning: assessment, benchmarks, priorities, and a shared vision.

A Note on AI and Today's Classroom

When this session first launched, the closing milestone was the return of artificial intelligence. That return has arrived in full. Generative AI tools now sit in nearly every district conversation about teaching, planning, and assessment. The questions this session asks have not changed, though. The right starting point is still purpose, not novelty, and the frameworks that follow apply to AI just as cleanly as they did to the laptop cart and the interactive whiteboard.

Want to keep going? Explore TCEA's current work on AI in education, including practical tools, ethics, and instructional design, on the TCEA TechNotes blog.
