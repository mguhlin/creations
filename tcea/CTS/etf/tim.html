Technology Integration Matrix

A common vocabulary that keeps attention where it belongs: on teaching and learning.

The TIM, developed by the Florida Center for Instructional Technology, describes and targets technology use to enhance learning. It emphasizes the pedagogical approach taken, not the devices used. Too often, technology becomes the tail wagging the dog; the TIM is built to prevent that.

▸

How It Is Built

The matrix crosses five characteristics of a meaningful learning environment with five levels of integration, creating 25 cells. Each cell is illustrated with classroom examples and video.

Five characteristics of meaningful learning

  • Active
  • Collaborative
  • Constructive
  • Authentic
  • Goal-directed

Five levels of integration

  • Entry
  • Adoption
  • Adaptation
  • Infusion
  • Transformation
