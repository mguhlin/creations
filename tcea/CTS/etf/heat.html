Theoretical Framework

HEAT Framework

Higher-order thinking, Engaged learning, Authentic connections, and Technology use.

Developed by Dr. Chris Moersch, HEAT is a student-learning framework about using technology to develop higher-order questioning and engagement through real-world connections. It provides practical descriptors for classroom walkthroughs, peer coaching, and lesson-plan evaluation.

▸

The Four Elements

Higher-order Thinking

Students apply, analyze, evaluate, and create rather than simply recall.

Engaged Learning

Students do the cognitive work themselves, directing and owning the task.

Authentic Connections

The work connects to real, relevant contexts beyond the assignment itself.

Technology Use

Technology supports and amplifies the first three, rather than standing in for them.

By "turning up the HEAT," students get the chance to transfer critical content to contextual situations with high engagement and relevance. The result is stronger achievement and stronger teaching.
