Theoretical Framework

LoTi Framework

Levels of Teaching Innovation: a progression from teacher-centered to student-centered practice.

Developed by Dr. Chris Moersch in 1994, LoTi focuses on the balance between instruction, assessment, and the effective use of digital resources to promote higher-order thinking, engaged learning, and authentic assessment. First introduced as the Levels of Technology Implementation, it was refreshed alongside the ISTE Standards and became the Levels of Teaching Innovation.

▸

What the Stages Track

The seven stages describe how teachers move:

  • from a teacher-centered to a student-centered environment;
  • from lower levels of understanding toward synthesis and problem-solving;
  • from simple classroom routines toward more complex ones;
  • from compliant use of digital tools toward self-directed use.

LoTi comes with a Digital-Age Survey, which gives teachers a snapshot of their level and a personalized development profile, and a classroom-observation "sniff test" for spotting how technology aligns with instruction.
