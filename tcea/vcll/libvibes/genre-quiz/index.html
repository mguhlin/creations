The Method

  
VIBES game plan for this build

  
    
      
  • VVision. Turn "I don't know what to read" into a 60-second interactive recommendation.
    • 
      
  • IInstruct. Give it your actual collection's titles so recommendations point at books you own.
    • 
      
  • BBuild. Build the question engine first; add the result-reveal animation and per-genre colors second.
    • 
      
  • EEvaluate. Click through every answer path; confirm every genre result fires and the titles are real.
    • 
      
  • SShip. Embed in your catalog page or post a QR code at the shelf ends.
    •
